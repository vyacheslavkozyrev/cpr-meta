# CPR Personas

Five user types across two axes: system role (permissions) and organizational level.

---

## Jane Smith — Individual Contributor (`Employee` role)

**Title**: Senior Software Engineer

**How she uses CPR**:
- Sets SMART goals with AI assistance based on skill assessments and career aspirations
- Collects ongoing feedback from peers and managers; tracks actionable items
- Monitors skill development progress with evidence and goal updates
- Builds a performance record of achievements for review and promotion discussions
- Earns and displays achievement badges for peer recognition

**Pain points**: Unclear promotion criteria; lack of actionable feedback; contributions invisible to decision-makers.

---

## Peter Morrison — People Manager (`PeopleManager` role)

**Title**: Engineering Manager / Principal Engineer · manages up to 10 people

**How he uses CPR**:
- Monitors team progress and aggregated feedback via team dashboard
- Creates and assigns SMART goals with AI assistance; tracks updates
- Runs team-level reports to identify skill gaps, training needs, and performance trends
- Reviews AI-analyzed feedback sentiment and quality metrics
- Approves and assigns achievement badges to team members

**Pain points**: No streamlined process to track career development across the team; hard to get consistent, measurable feedback quality.

---

## Tim Goldman — Director (`Director` role)

**Title**: Director (senior leadership)

**How he uses CPR**:
- Reviews org- and department-level analytics to detect trends and promotion-ready candidates
- Uses calibration tools and standardized review templates to ensure cross-team consistency
- Monitors manager-submitted evidence and feedback quality; requests clarifications
- Exports datasets for calibration sessions with senior leadership and HR
- Leverages predictive modeling for retention risk and succession planning

**Pain points**: Bias and inconsistency in promotion decisions; managers lack skills for structured, objective feedback.

---

## Catherine Lumber — Solution Owner (`SolutionOwner` role)

**Title**: Solution Owner / Project Manager

**How she uses CPR**:
- Reviews team skill maps and past feedback to make staffing decisions
- Provides structured cross-functional feedback to people leaders
- Uses reports to identify training needs and development opportunities
- Facilitates peer recognition and badges for project contributions

**Pain points**: Difficulty matching skills to project needs; limited visibility into individual readiness for additional responsibilities.

---

## Steve Miller — HR Administrator (`Administrator` role)

**Title**: HR Manager / HR Administrator

**How he uses CPR**:
- Configures retention schedules, export formats, and recurring exports for legal/payroll
- Runs compliance reports (promotion readiness, review completeness, calibration summaries)
- Reviews audit logs and access histories; investigates anomalies
- Sets and verifies role-based access controls and approval workflows
- Manages HRIS and learning platform data synchronization

**Pain points**: Incomplete audit logs; manual time-consuming report generation; unclear access controls.
